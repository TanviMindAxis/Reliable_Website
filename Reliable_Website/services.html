Our Core Services

We provide highly accurate and reliable geospatial data for modern infrastructure, utilizing state-of-the-art surveying equipment and industry-leading methodologies.

Topographical Survey

Detailed mapping of natural and man-made features. We produce highly accurate contour maps, spot levels, and comprehensive baseline data essential for preliminary design and detailed engineering.

Request Survey

DGPS / GNSS Control

Establishment of primary and secondary geodetic control networks using dual-frequency GNSS receivers. Crucial for long linear projects like highways, railways, and metro rail systems.

Request Survey

Total Station Survey

High-precision detailed surveys for construction staking, as-built mapping, structural alignment, and complex boundary demarcations using advanced Total Stations.

Request Survey

RTK Drone Mapping

Rapid aerial photogrammetry over vast terrains. Delivers high-resolution orthomosaics, Digital Elevation Models (DEM), and 3D surface models with absolute accuracy.

Request Survey

LiDAR 3D Scanning

Aerial and terrestrial laser scanning for dense vegetation penetration and complex structural modeling. Outputs highly dense and extremely accurate 3D point clouds.

Request Survey
