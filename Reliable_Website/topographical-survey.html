Technical Standards & Deliverables

A Topographical Survey provides the fundamental baseline for all architectural design, civil engineering, and infrastructure development. TECHNICAL FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Specific queries regarding our Topographical Survey methodology and deliverables.

Why is a topographical survey essential before construction?
A topographical survey reveals the exact surface relief, slopes, and existing site obstacles. It prevents expensive engineering design mistakes, foundation rework, and inaccurate earthwork budget estimations.
What contour intervals do you generate?
We standardly generate 0.5m or 1.0m contour intervals, and can deliver ultra-fine 0.2m intervals for sensitive grading, drainage design, or golf course developments.
What file formats will I receive?
Deliverables include standard AutoCAD (.DWG, .DXF), 3D surfaces (.TIN, .LandXML), CSV coordinate tables (Easting, Northing, Elevation, Description), and stamped PDF layout sheets.
How do you accurately survey heavily vegetated or wooded land?
For dense tree canopy or jungle areas, we employ a hybrid methodology combining penetration ground-strike LiDAR with electronic total stations and traverse clearing lines, ensuring true bare-earth terrain capture beneath foliage.
How are site boundary markers and natural drainage features identified?
We map all physical boundary markers (fences, compound walls, stones), natural streams, nallas, water bodies, and catchment flow directions with spot level elevations, clearly flagged on CAD layers for hydrology and site planning.
Can the topographical survey be tied into Mean Sea Level (MSL) benchmarks?
Yes. We tie all ground elevation levels to the nearest Survey of India (SOI) Mean Sea Level (MSL) benchmark or established geodetic vertical datum using high-precision digital levels and dual-frequency GNSS.
